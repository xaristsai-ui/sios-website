About SIOS Consulting

Impartial reports. Precise designs. Plain findings.

SIOS Consulting is a team of building designers and expert building consultants with more than a decade of inspecting, reporting and designing across Australia.

What we hold to

Four commitments.

Impartial.

We report what we find, whoever commissions the work. A finding does not change with the client.

Thorough.

Every wall, room and boundary in scope is inspected, photographed and logged against the plan.

Plain.

Sentences are complete and short. A technical term appears only when it is the right word for the reader.

Documented.

We record what is true and nothing more. A finding that cannot be traced to its evidence is not issued.

Who we work for

Five kinds of client.

Each reads a report for a different reason. We write it so that all five can rely on the same document.

Insurance companies.

Inspections, scopes of works and written findings for claims and repairs.

Loss adjusters.

Damage assessed, separated by cause and set out so that the adjustment can be defended.

Legal professionals.

Expert reports for disputes, prepared to stand up to challenge.

Builders.

Condition records before work starts and a second look at quality while it runs.

Homeowners.

A plain answer on the state of a home, and design and permits when the answer leads to work.

Approach

How a job runs.

Seven stages, in this order, on every job. Each ends with something written that the client can check and a fully completed project ready to move in.

Design

The Seven Steps of Design.

1

Feasibility & Pre-Design.

We test the idea against the site, the planning controls and the budget before design starts.

2

Concept Design.

First layouts are drawn on one plan so the options can be compared.

3

Design Development.

The preferred concept is refined and the detail is resolved.

4

Town Planning (if required).

Where the project needs a planning permit, we prepare the application and liaise with council.

5

Working Drawings & Building Permit.

Construction drawings are completed, and the building permit is lodged and tracked to issue.

6

Builder Appointment & Construction.

The documents go to builders for pricing. Once a builder is appointed, construction begins.

7

Project Completion.

The project reaches completion and is handed over.

Reporting

The Four Steps of Reporting.

1

Building Inspection.

We inspect the site and record what is there on the day.

2

Photographs & Site Notes.

Dated photographs and site notes are logged against the plan.

3

Report or Scope Completion.

The report or scope of works is written in plain English with the evidence attached.

4

Post Submission Support.

We answer questions about the report after it has been submitted.
